# Product overview

[Docs](../README.md) / Product / Overview

Hyperlocal is a multi-vendor delivery marketplace. The platform owner onboards local stores,
customers order from stores that deliver to their address, and riders deliver the orders.

## Verticals

Food, grocery, pharmacy, fashion, electronics and local services. The launch covers food
and grocery; see the [decision log](../decisions/README.md).

## How the platform earns

1. **Category-wise commission:** a percentage of every order line, set per category.
2. **Vendor subscription plans:** a monthly fee per vendor, with plan-based limits and perks.

Details: [Monetization](monetization.md).

## Who uses it

Customers, vendors, riders, admins and support agents. Details: [Roles](roles.md).
